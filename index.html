Thrissur district beaches

Popular and underrated beaches along the district's coastline, with their locations on the map.

Notes on the data

  • Coordinates marked "official" come from the Kerala Tourism listings for Chavakkad and Snehatheeram. The rest are estimates from the coastline and must be confirmed on site with GPS (edit lat and lng in BEACHES).
  • "Popular" and "underrated" are qualitative labels. Replace them with the district’s footfall figures (DTPC counts, panchayat records) when available. Beach names and the list itself should be confirmed with DTPC Thrissur.
  • The monsoon months (roughly June to September) bring rough seas and rip currents along the Kerala coast. Check current IMD and KSDMA advisories before opening any beach for visitors.
