Thrissur district beaches

Popular and underrated beaches along the district's coastline, with their locations on the map.

Notes on the data

  • Coordinates are approximate, placed from general knowledge of the coastline. Confirm each one on site with GPS and edit lat and lng in BEACHES.
  • "Popular" and "underrated" are qualitative labels for planning. Replace them with the district's own footfall figures (DTPC counts, panchayat records) when available.
  • The monsoon months (roughly June to September) bring rough seas and rip currents along the Kerala coast. Check current IMD and KSDMA advisories before opening any beach for visitors.
