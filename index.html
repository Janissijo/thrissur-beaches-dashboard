Notes on Data & Field Verification

  • Coordinates marked "official" originate from official Kerala Tourism / DTPC listings (e.g. Chavakkad, Snehatheeram, Munakkal). Remaining locations are approximate coastal estimations verified via satellite bounds.
  • "Popular" and "Underrated" classifications are qualitative. Replace with official footfall metrics from DTPC Thrissur or local Grama Panchayats when available.
  • Monsoon conditions (June to September) bring turbulent waters and strong rip currents along the Arabian coast. Always consult live IMD and KSDMA safety advisories prior to beach activities.
